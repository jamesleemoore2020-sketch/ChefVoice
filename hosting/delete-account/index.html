Delete your ChefVoice account

Permanently remove your ChefVoice cloud account and Community data, even if you no longer have the app installed.

What this removes

  • Your ChefVoice profile, published recipes, photos and video
  • Likes, follows, bookmarks, comments and messages you sent
  • Any Live broadcasting history tied to your account
  • Your sign-in itself — you will be signed out everywhere

This does not affect cooking recipes saved only on your phone — those live locally on-device and are unrelated to this cloud account.

Step 1 · Sign in

Use the same email and password you use to sign in to ChefVoice on Android.

Step 2 · Confirm deletion

This cannot be undone. Type DELETE below to confirm.

Confirm it's you

For security, re-enter your password to finish permanently deleting your account.

Account deleted

Your ChefVoice cloud account has been permanently deleted.
