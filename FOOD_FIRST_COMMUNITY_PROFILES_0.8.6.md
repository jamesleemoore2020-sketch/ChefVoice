# ChefVoice Android v0.8.6 — Food-First Community + Chef Profiles

This cumulative source starts from v0.8.5 Followed Live Alerts and adds the approved social-layout pass.

- Community is finished-dish-photo first. Chef avatar/name lives inside the upper-left of the meal image.
- Chef identity from Community, recipe detail, and Live opens a public Chef Profile.
- Public Chef Profile shows bio, follower count, public recipe count, Follow/Following, Message, active Live, and a finished-dish recipe grid.
- Follower identities are not exposed; counts only.
- Live rooms prioritize followed chefs.
- Follow and heart/fire/clap reactions are integrated over the Live viewing area.
- Protected WebRTC transport, deterministic cooking parser, original audio, Second Pass, App Check staging, and Storage privacy are unchanged.

Real-device acceptance is pending until this build is compiled, installed, and tested on Android.
